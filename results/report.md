# Proposition X — Simulation Report

Simulated ballot: cap third-party delivery-app fees (DoorDash, Uber Eats) at 15%.

## Overall result

- **90.0% Yes** (27 Yes / 3 No, 30 agents)
- Real SF Prop F (2021): **60.8% Yes**
- **Delta: +29.2 percentage points** vs. reality

## Top reasons to vote YES

1. **Fairness / curb price-gouging** — 14 agent(s)
2. **Protect restaurant workers & drivers** — 6 agent(s)
3. **Support local restaurants / small business** — 6 agent(s)

## Top reasons to vote NO

1. **My own delivery costs would rise** — 1 agent(s)
2. **Other reasons** — 1 agent(s)
3. **Tight budget / fixed income** — 1 agent(s)

## Most interesting response

**Chen Hassan** (agent 3) voted **Yes**:

> I gotta think about the local restaurants and workers who struggle to make ends meet, they deserve some protection from these big apps squeezing 'em for every penny.

_Why it stands out:_ Their profile leans cost-focused/skeptical (expected-Yes score -1.0), yet they voted Yes — the sharpest personality-vote mismatch in the run.

## Extras — experiments

### Memory (5.1): grounding votes in lived experience

- Memory OFF: 100.0% Yes
- Memory ON: 90.0% Yes (-10.0 pp)

### Social influence (5.2): round-1 vs round-2

- Round 1 (private): 90.0% Yes
- Round 2 (saw neighbors): 90.0% Yes
- Agents who changed their vote: 4/30

### Second scenario (5.5): $5 credit offered to vote No

- Before offer: 90.0% Yes
- After $5 credit: 23.3% Yes
- Agents swayed by the offer: 24/30
