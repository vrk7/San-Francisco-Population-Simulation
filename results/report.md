# Proposition X — Simulation Report

Simulated ballot: cap third-party delivery-app fees (DoorDash, Uber Eats) at 15%.

## Overall result

- **96.7% Yes** (29 Yes / 1 No, 30 agents)
- Real SF Prop F (2021): **60.8% Yes**
- **Delta: +35.9 percentage points** vs. reality

## Top reasons to vote YES

1. **Protect restaurant workers & drivers** — 12 agent(s)
2. **Fairness / curb price-gouging** — 10 agent(s)
3. **Support local restaurants / small business** — 6 agent(s)

## Top reasons to vote NO

1. **My own delivery costs would rise** — 1 agent(s)

## Most interesting response

**Aisha Wong** (agent 21) voted **Yes**:

> I've seen how those delivery apps hurt small businesses like the cafe that closed down on my block, and I'm willing to pay a little extra myself if it means keeping our local restaurants alive.

_Why it stands out:_ Their profile leans cost-focused/skeptical (expected-Yes score -1.5), yet they voted Yes — the sharpest personality-vote mismatch in the run.
